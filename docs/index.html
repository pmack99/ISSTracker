iOS app

Watch the space station live. Never miss a pass.

Pass alerts & live map — track the ISS on a real-time map, predict visible flyovers for your location, and get reminded before a pass begins.

Built for iPhone (iOS 17+). App Store link will appear here when the app is published.

Live map & station data

Hybrid map with smooth ISS motion, optional follow mode, plus dock panels for orbit, crew on board, and NASA cabin telemetry.

Overhead passes

Search by city, ZIP, or current location. See visible passes for the next 10 days with elevation, direction, and duration.

Notifications

Local reminders before passes you care about — no account required.

Live Activity

Optional pass countdown on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island on supported iPhones when you enable it for a pass.

More in the app

  • Crew and cabin panels on the Live tab
  • Compass assist during a pass
  • Saved places and search history on device
  • NASA ISS photo gallery

About

ISS Tracker continues a web project from the UCF Coding Bootcamp (Preston Mack, Christine Deer, Sarah Sefcik, Jennifer Gibson), rebuilt as a native iPhone app with MapKit and SwiftUI.
